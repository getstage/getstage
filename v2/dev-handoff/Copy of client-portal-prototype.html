Company Overview
Acme is a mid-stage B2B SaaS company providing workflow automation tools for operations teams. Founded in 2021, they serve ~2,000 companies. Their current brand feels generic and doesn't differentiate them in an increasingly crowded market.
Market Landscape
The workflow automation space is experiencing rapid growth, projected to reach $26B by 2027. The market is becoming increasingly fragmented with both horizontal platforms (Zapier, Make) and vertical solutions targeting specific industries. Key trends include AI-native automation and a shift from feature-based to outcome-based positioning.
Opportunities
1. Position around outcomes for ops teams, not automation features 2. Lean into the mid-market gap between Zapier and enterprise tools 3. Develop a distinctive visual brand that signals modern and trustworthy 4. Build thought leadership around "operational excellence" 5. Invest in community and ecosystem early
Key Insights
• Operations teams feel underserved by tools built for marketing and sales • Brand trust matters more than feature count in enterprise buying decisions • AI-native automation is the next battleground • "Reliability" and "ease of handoff" are the most valued qualities • Top competitors invest heavily in educational content, not just features
Goals & KPIs
Redesign acmestudio.com to increase demo requests by 40% within 6 months. Target metrics: demo conversion rate 4.5%, bounce rate below 45%, average session duration above 2 minutes, organic traffic growth of 30% in 6 months.
User Journeys
Three primary journeys identified: 1. The Evaluator — Operations manager researching solutions. Enters via Google/blog, needs to quickly understand what Acme does and reach a demo form within 3 clicks. 2. The Decision Maker — VP/C-level comparing vendors. Needs ROI data, enterprise features, and security/compliance information. 3. The Existing User — Current customer exploring upgrades. Needs clear upgrade path and feature comparison.
Sitemap
/ — Homepage (hero, value props, social proof, CTA) /product — Product overview with feature deep-dives /solutions — Use-case pages by team type /pricing — Transparent pricing with comparison table /customers — Case studies and testimonials /resources — Blog, guides, webinars, templates /demo — Demo request form
Conversion Approach
Primary conversion: Demo request (gated by form). Secondary: Free trial signup (ungated, no credit card). CTA hierarchy: "Book a Demo" in header, hero, and page bottoms. "Start Free Trial" on pricing and product pages. Nurture path: Blog readers → email capture → drip sequence → demo invitation. Target: 15% of email subscribers convert to demo within 30 days.
